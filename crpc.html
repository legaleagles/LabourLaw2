Important Notice
CrPC → BNSS: What Changed?
The Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023 replaced CrPC 1973 from July 1, 2024.

✅ For your Osmania University exam — syllabus still says CrPC. Learn CrPC section numbers.
✅ For your career (courts, practice) — use BNSS numbers from July 2024 onwards.
✅ In exams — you can mention "now codified as Sec ___ BNSS 2023" after the CrPC section for extra marks.
