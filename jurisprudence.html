Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Austin\'s Command Theory, Savigny\'s Volksgeist, Pound\'s Social Engineering & Jural Postulates, Original vs Declaratory Precedent, Hohfeld\'s correlative pairs, Corpus + Animus (possession) — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Jurisprudence exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Jurists
John Austin (Command Theory, Analytical School) and Friedrich Carl von Savigny (Volksgeist, Historical School) anchor the two great foundational, contrasting theories of law\'s nature. Also essential: Roscoe Pound (Sociological School, Social Engineering) and H.L.A. Hart (Primary/Secondary Rules).
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Salmond\'s 3 branches (Analytical/Historical/Ethical). 5 Schools of Jurisprudence. Pound\'s 3 categories of interests (Individual/Public/Social). 5 requirements for valid custom. Hohfeld\'s 4 correlative pairs.
