Exam Tips
🔥 Most Important Topics 2024-25
Sources of IL (Article 38) — appears EVERY year as 15M essay. Diplomatic Immunity (VCDR 1961) — appeared 2022, 2024, 2025. ICJ Jurisdiction — appeared 2023, 2024, 2025. Law of the Sea (UNCLOS) — 2024, 2025. State Succession — 2021, 2022, 2025.
⚠️ Twisted Questions to Watch
Can a diplomat be arrested for murder? NO — Article 31 VCDR gives complete criminal immunity. Can an individual sue in ICJ? NO — only States. Is conquest of territory valid today? NO — UN Charter Article 2(4). What is the difference between EEZ and Territorial Sea? Full sovereignty vs sovereign rights.
💡 Memory Hooks
Article 38 Sources = TCGJ-T (Treaties, Custom, General Principles, Judicial decisions, Teachings). Maritime Zones = I-T-C-E-C-H (Internal, Territorial 12NM, Contiguous 24NM, EEZ 200NM, Continental Shelf, High Seas). State Succession = MDSCN (Merger, Dissolution, Secession, Cession, New Independence).
