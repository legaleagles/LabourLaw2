Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Copyright Infringement (every year), TRIPS Agreement, Passing Off, Compulsory Licensing (Bayer v Natco), Moral Rights (Section 57), GI Registration — these appear in EVERY Osmania University IPR exam. Prepare all six first.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
R.G. Anand v Delux Films (Idea-Expression), Novartis v Union of India (Section 3d), Bayer v Natco (Compulsory Licence), Cadila v Cadila (Deceptive Similarity), Amarnath Sehgal (Moral Rights). Five cases = 90% of case questions.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Patent term = 20 years. Copyright = Life + 60 years (films = 60 years from publication). TM duration = 10 years renewable. Design = 10 + 5 = 15 years. Compulsory licence after 3 years. Section 63 criminal = 6 months to 3 years + Rs 50,000 fine.
