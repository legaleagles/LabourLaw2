Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

What we record when you open a page

  • The page you opened, the time, and the site that sent you here (if any).
  • Your IP address, and the approximate city and internet provider that it points to.
  • Your browser and device type (for example “Android phone, Chrome”), screen size, language and time zone.
  • A random id stored in your browser, so repeat visits from the same device can be counted once. It contains nothing about you.

We also use Google Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate.

Why

To see which notes and tools are actually used, fix pages that people leave quickly, and spot misuse such as automated scraping or attacks.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, phone or email to read the site.
  • We don't sell or share this information. Only the site administrator can see it.
  • Visit records are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Things you type

Questions you ask Vidura and documents you upload to a tool are sent to the AI service that answers them. Don't include personal details you don't want processed. Feedback you send reaches the administrator; your contact details are never published.

Your choices

Clearing your browser's site data removes the random id. Blocking scripts or using your browser's tracking protection stops the visit record. To ask about or delete anything we hold, use the Feedback button on the home page.
