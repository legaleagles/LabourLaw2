Three ways to revise

📖 Read · the night before

Read each cluster once. Say every anchor aloud and link each letter to its heading. Tick the cluster when done; your progress stays on your phone.

🙈 Test me · active recall

Answers hide behind stripes. Say the heading or point from the anchor, then tap to check. Recalling before you look is what makes it stick.

🦴 Skeleton · on the way

Only anchors, headings, sections, numbers and case names: the answer plans you will write as side headings. Ten minutes per paper.

How to use quick revision (2 minutes to read)
  1. The night before: 📖 Read. Go cluster by cluster. Say each 🧠 anchor aloud, then read the headings it stands for and the highlighted key words. Tap “Mark this cluster as revised” as you finish.
  2. Next: 🙈 Test me. Answers are covered with stripes. Look at the anchor and its letters, say each heading and point aloud, then tap to check. Whatever you missed, read once more.
  3. On the way to the centre: 🦴 Skeleton. Only anchors, headings, sections, numbers and case names. Expand each heading in your head; that is your answer plan.
  4. In the exam: write the anchor's headings first as your numbered side headings, then fill each with its key words, section and case. 🔥 shows how many times the question has been asked.
  5. No network at the centre? Download the 📱 phone PDF before you leave. It works offline.
