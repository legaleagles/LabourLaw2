Why this is safe to use
Most free PDF tools upload your file to their server to process it. This tool doesn't — the merging and splitting happens entirely in your phone or computer's browser using open-source libraries, and your files are never transmitted anywhere. Good for personal documents, certificates, or anything you'd rather not upload to an unknown server.
