Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Actus Reus & Mens Rea, the three tiers of incapacity (under 7 / 7-12 / unsoundness), Section 34-44 private defence framework, the five Exceptions to murder, Grievous Hurt categories, Kidnapping vs Abduction — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Law of Crimes-I exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Darshan Singh v State of Punjab (2010 — reasonable apprehension is enough, no mathematical precision required) and Katta Surendra v State of Andhra Pradesh (2008 — Exception 2 requires a genuine prior right, good faith, no premeditation). These two cover most case-based questions in this subject.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
BNS effective 1 July 2024, 358 sections/20 chapters. Section 20 (under 7, absolute) vs 21 (7-12, qualified) vs 22 (unsoundness). Sections 34-44 (private defence). Section 100 (culpable homicide), 101 (murder, 5 exceptions), 103(2) (NEW mob-lynching provision).
