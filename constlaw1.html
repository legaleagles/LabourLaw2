Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Article 14 reasonable classification test, all six Article 19(1) freedoms, the Golden Triangle (14+19+21), Basic Structure Doctrine, Article 32 writs, DPSP classification (Socialistic/Gandhian/Liberal-Intellectual) — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Constitutional Law-I exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Kesavananda Bharati v State of Kerala (Basic Structure Doctrine, 13-judge bench, 1973) and Maneka Gandhi v Union of India (Golden Triangle, fair procedure under Article 21, overruling A.K. Gopalan, 1978). These two cover the vast majority of case-based questions in this subject.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
26 January 1950 = Constitution commencement. 24 April 1973 = Kesavananda Bharati judgment. Article 21-A (86th Amendment, 2002) = Right to Education, ages 6-14. Article 22 = 24-hour production before magistrate. 42nd Amendment 1976 = added Socialist/Secular + Fundamental Duties.
