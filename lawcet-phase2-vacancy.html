Phase 2 Seat Vacancy Estimator

Cross-references each college's official sanctioned intake against real Phase 1 allotment data, so you can see which colleges likely have the most seats actually open for Phase 2.

📋 How This Is Calculated

Estimated Vacancy = Convener Quota Intake − Phase 1 Allotted Candidates.

Convener Intake (not total College Intake) is the correct baseline — it's specifically the seat pool that LAWCET counseling itself allots against. Management/NRI quota seats are filled separately by colleges outside this process and were never part of what Phase 1/Phase 2 counseling is competing over.

Source data: the official 2026-27 LLB 3-Year Degree Course college/intake list, and the official TG LAWCET Phase 1 allotment results.
⚠️ This is an estimate, not an official seat matrix. Many colleges show "Fully Allotted" — Phase 1 allotting slightly at or beyond the convener quota number is normal counseling practice (anticipating some allottees won't join), not a data error. Actual Phase 2 vacancy depends on how many Phase 1 allottees actually joined vs. were just allotted, plus any cancellations or upgrades between phases — only the counseling authority can confirm final numbers. Always verify on the official TG LAWCET website before deciding. Two colleges show "No Data" because no matching Phase 1 record was found for them — this does not necessarily mean zero seats were filled there.
