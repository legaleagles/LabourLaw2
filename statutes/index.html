Exam Tips
🔥 Most Repeated Topics (OU Exam Pattern)
Golden Rule + Mischief Rule comparison (15M) — appears almost every year. Strict Construction of Penal Statutes (15M) — very high frequency. Effect of Repeal + Section 6 GCA (6M + problems). Ejusdem Generis (6M) — Vegetables problem is a classic. External vs Internal Aids (15M or 6M). Harmonious Construction (6M).
⚠️ High-Value Problem Questions
Vegetables + Sales Tax → Always Ejusdem Generis. Soliciting from balcony → Always Mischief Rule (Smith v Hughes). Repealed Act during trial → Always Section 6 GCA. Mobile phone while stationary → Mischief Rule vs Literal. Penal statute — two interpretations → Rule of Lenity — ACQUIT. Use IRAC in every problem for full marks.
💡 Memory Hooks — Cases
Heydon's Case 1584 → Mischief Rule (4 Questions). Grey v Pearson 1857 → Golden Rule. Sussex Peerage 1844 → Literal Rule. Smith v Hughes 1960 → Mischief applied. Fisher v Bell 1961 → Literal Rule (shop display). R v Allen 1872 → Golden Rule (bigamy). CIT v Sodra Devi → Strict construction penal/tax.
