⚠️ Read This Before You Rely On The Result
Age rules change with EVERY new exam notification and are set independently by each conducting body. This tool reflects rules researched as of mid-2026 and is a planning aid, NOT a substitute for the official notification of the specific exam year you're applying to. Where we could not confirm exact figures with confidence, we say so explicitly rather than guess.

Sources & Method
Age is calculated as of the date you specify (most exams use 1st August of the exam year, or the notification's own cutoff — always confirm the exact date from the official notification). Category relaxations shown are the STANDARD relaxation for that category on the GENERAL upper limit; some exams also offer PwBD, ex-servicemen, and other relaxations not shown here — check the official notification for your specific situation.
