The whole subject is one advocate's life, in five stops. Remember the stops and you can talk about any topic.

One line on ethics: Professional ethics is the set of rules of conduct an advocate must follow towards the court, the client, the opponent, colleagues and society. A lawyer is an officer of the court, so law is a noble profession, not a business.
The trick for 60 casesAll 60 cases grow from one trunk: a complaint against an advocate. It splits into seven branches. Learn the branches, then tap any leaf to read its one-line story and result.

Read the question, say the answer out loud, then tap to check.

4 days, about 30 minutes a day

  1. Day 1 · Journey. Read the five stops. Close the page and say the five stops in order with one section each.
  2. Day 2 · Star cases + the Punished door. Narrate the six star cases out loud. Note the punishment in each.
  3. Day 3 · Other doors. Just learn which door each case goes through. Read the door titles, not every line.
  4. Day 4 · Viva cards. Go through all the cards twice. Practise the opening "What is in your record?" answer.
  5. Monday morning. Five stops, the seven lamps mnemonic, and two star cases. Nothing new.
In the viva: start every answer with the section and a one-line meaning, then stop. Examiners stop you when you're right. If you don't know a case, say "That case came under [door]: the complaint failed because…" and give the general rule.
