Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Article 74 aid & advice, Money Bill procedure, Governor\'s discretionary powers, S.R. Bommai\'s floor test principle, the Collegium/NJAC history, National vs President\'s Rule vs Financial Emergency — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Constitutional Law-II exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
S.R. Bommai v Union of India (1994 — 9-judge bench, Article 356 justiciable, floor test, federalism + secularism = Basic Structure) and SCAORA v Union of India / Fourth Judges Case (2015 — NJAC struck down, judicial independence = Basic Structure). Also know the full First/Second/Third Judges Case sequence.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Article 74 (aid & advice), 110 (Money Bill), 226 vs 32 (writ scope), 352/356/360 (three emergencies), 44th Amendment 1978 (armed rebellion, Art 20/21 protection), 99th Amendment 2014 (NJAC, struck down 2015). Financial Emergency: NEVER invoked.
