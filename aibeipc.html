Exam Strategy

⚡ Trainer's Top Priority
Master the IPC-to-BNS correspondence table -- including the S.303 trap (murder-by-life-convict under IPC, THEFT under BNS, same number, totally different offence). Then drill Section 101's four clauses and five exceptions until you can spot which exception a scenario fits instantly.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Mithu v. State of Punjab (1983, struck down mandatory death) and Virsa Singh v. State of Punjab (1958, objective sufficiency test). Also know Bachan Singh ("rarest of rare"), Basdev v. State of Pepsu (intention vs knowledge), and K.M. Nanavati (time gap defeats "sudden" provocation).
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Culpable Homicide: 299=100. Murder: 300=101. Punishment: 302=103. Attempt formula: up to 1/2 the max term. Conspiracy: mere agreement suffices ONLY for death/life/2+yr offences. Consent exception (S.5): deceased must be above 18.
