Flight Delay Rights
2 hrs · 6 hrs · Overnight · Free meals · Refund
▼
ENTITLEMENT
2+ Hour Delay — Free Meals Mandatory
If your domestic flight is delayed by 2 hours or more and you checked in on time — the airline must provide free meals and refreshments. This is not optional. Not a gesture of goodwill. It is a legal obligation.
💬 Exactly What to Say
→"My flight has been delayed over 2 hours. I have a valid boarding pass and checked in on time. Under DGCA regulations I am legally entitled to complimentary meals. Please provide meal vouchers now."
→If they say "not our policy" — say: "This is DGCA CAR Section 3 Series M Part IV. It is Indian law, not a policy. I will file on AirSewa immediately if you refuse."
→Collect the voucher slip. Take a photo. It is evidence if you need to escalate.
Legal basis: DGCA Civil Aviation Requirements (CAR) Section 3, Series M, Part IV — Handling of Cancelled, Delayed & Diverted Flights
ENTITLEMENT
6+ Hour Delay — Full Refund OR Rebooking (Your Choice)
If your flight is delayed 6+ hours, the airline must offer you a choice: full refund of your ticket OR rebooking on the next available flight at no extra charge. The choice is yours — not the airline's. For overnight delays (departure 20:00–03:00) they must also provide free hotel and airport transfers.
Full Refund + Hotel if overnight
STEP BY STEP
1
Go to airline customer service desk the moment 6+ hour delay is announced.
2
State: "I want a full refund" or "I want rebooking on the next flight." You pick — not them.
3
For overnight — demand hotel accommodation and transfer in writing before leaving.
4
Photograph the delay announcement board showing time — this is your evidence.
5
If refused — file on AirSewa app or airsewa.gov.in immediately. DGCA responds fast.
Legal basis: DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV, Para 3.3 & 3.7
IMPORTANT LIMIT
No Cash Compensation for Delay (Unlike EU)
India does not mandate cash compensation purely for delays (unlike EU rules that give €250–600). Indian rights on delay: free meals, hotel, and refund/rebooking option. Weather and force majeure delays are exempt — airline owes you basic care only.
⚠️ Don't Accept Less Than You're Entitled To
Many passengers accept a small meal voucher and go home. For 6+ hour delays, you have the right to a full ticket refund — not just vouchers. Exercise it before you leave the airport.
ENTITLEMENT
2 Free Phone Calls or Emails During Any Delay
During any significant delay, the airline must allow you up to 2 free phone calls, emails or faxes to inform family or make alternate arrangements.
💬 How to Use This
→"I need to make 2 free calls to inform family of this delay. Under DGCA rules the airline must facilitate this. Please arrange access now."
→If no phone — ask for lounge WiFi access or a lounge pass instead.
Legal basis: DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV, Para 3.2(c)

Cancellation Rights
Always: refund or alternate · Compensation · Refund timelines

ENTITLEMENT
Always: Full Refund OR Alternate Flight — Your Choice
Whenever a flight is cancelled, the airline must offer you: full refund OR alternate flight at no extra cost. The choice belongs to you — they cannot force you onto an alternate flight if you want your money back.

Legal basis: DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV, Para 3.3
ENTITLEMENT
Refund Timelines — They Cannot Delay
Refunds have legally mandated timelines: Cash — immediately · Credit/debit card — within 7 working days · Travel agent booking — within 14 working days. Delay beyond these limits is itself a DGCA violation.
IF REFUND IS DELAYED
1
Email airline's nodal officer. Keep screenshot with timestamp.
2
File on AirSewa (airsewa.gov.in) — select "Refund not received."
3
File Consumer Complaint at District Consumer Commission. No lawyer needed up to ₹50 lakhs.
4
Credit card users: initiate a chargeback with your bank — very effective for airline refunds.
Legal basis: DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV, Para 3.8; Consumer Protection Act 2019 §38
NEGOTIATE
Cancelled at Airport — Get More Than Just Refund
If you are already at the airport when cancellation is announced — you also get free meals and refreshments while waiting, in addition to your refund or rebooking rights.
💬 Power Script at Counter
→"My flight was cancelled and I am already at the airport. Under DGCA CAR 3.7.1(a), I am entitled to free meals and refreshments now. Please provide meal vouchers."
→"Additionally, since this cancellation notice was under 24 hours, I am entitled to compensation under CAR 3.3.2. I want that confirmed in writing before I board any alternate."
→Note the name and employee ID of every staff member you speak to. Time-stamp everything.

Denied Boarding / Overbooking
Up to ₹20,000 · Volunteers first · Demand cash not vouchers
▼
ENTITLEMENT
Airlines Must Ask for Volunteers First
Airlines legally overbook flights. When they have too many passengers, DGCA mandates they ask for volunteers first — people willing to give up their seat in exchange for benefits. Only if there are not enough volunteers can they involuntarily deny boarding.
💡 Volunteer Strategy
→If you have flexibility — negotiate before agreeing. Say "What are you offering?" Then wait. Counter with more. Airlines want volunteers urgently.
→Get everything in writing — printed voucher or email — before giving up your seat. Verbal promises at the gate disappear.
→Confirm: which flight, what time, what class, and any cash compensation. Get all on paper.

IF DENIED BOARDING INVOLUNTARILY
1
Do not leave the gate area. Ask: "Why am I denied boarding? Is this overbooking?" Get it in writing.
2

3
Also demand: free meals, refreshments, and hotel if overnight wait required.
4
If refused — file on AirSewa immediately. DGCA has issued fines of ₹10 lakh+ for such violations.
Legal basis: DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV, Para 3.4 — Denied Boarding Compensation

Baggage — Lost, Delayed or Damaged
File PIR first · ₹20,000 domestic · ₹1.72 lakh international
▼
CRITICAL — DO THIS FIRST
File PIR Before Leaving the Baggage Hall
If your bag is missing — do NOT exit the baggage hall. Go straight to the airline's baggage desk and file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR). Get the 10-character World Tracer ID. Without a PIR filed before you exit — most claims fail at this single point.
⚠️ Once You Exit — Your Position Weakens Dramatically
The airline treats exit from baggage hall as acceptance of delivery. Even if you are exhausted or in a hurry — file PIR first. Everything else can wait.
Legal basis: DGCA CAR; Montreal Convention 1999, Article 31 — Notice must be given in writing

CLAIM PROCESS
1
File PIR at baggage desk — collect 10-character World Tracer ID.
2
Track bag on airline portal using surname + World Tracer ID.
3
Bag not found after 21 days = officially lost. Submit written claim with PIR copy + baggage tag + boarding pass.
4
Keep receipts for emergency purchases (clothes, medicine) due to delayed bag — airline is liable for these.
5
If airline refuses — file Consumer Complaint + AirSewa simultaneously.
💡 Prevention Tips
→Never pack jewellery, cash, medicines, passports or electronics in checked baggage. DGCA and Montreal Convention explicitly limit liability for valuables.
→Photograph your bag and its contents before travel — establishes value for compensation claims.
→For damaged baggage — photograph damage at the baggage hall before leaving. File PIR even for damaged bags.
Legal basis: Montreal Convention 1999, Articles 17–22; DGCA CAR; Revised SDR value per IMF Dec 2024

Senior Citizens & Special Needs Passengers
Wheelchair free · Priority boarding · Visually impaired · Guide dog
▼
WHEELCHAIR — FREE BY LAW
Wheelchair Assistance is Mandatory and Free
At every Indian airport, wheelchair assistance is free of charge. Charging for it is illegal. Three levels: WCHR (can climb stairs), WCHS (cannot climb stairs), WCHC (fully immobile — needs cabin seat assistance). Book at least 48 hours before departure via airline website or call centre. Non-compliance penalty: up to ₹1 crore per incident.
🚨 If They Try to Charge You
→"Wheelchair assistance is mandatorily free under DGCA CAR Section 3 Series M Part I. Charging for this is a violation. I will file a DGCA complaint immediately if you proceed."
→In 2024, DGCA issued show-cause notices to airlines charging ₹1,500 for wheelchair. All had to refund. This right is enforced.
Legal basis: DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part I — Carriage by Air of Persons with Disability and Persons with Reduced Mobility (2014, revised 2022)
SENIOR CITIZENS — 60+
Senior Citizen Rights at Airports
Priority boarding is mandatory for passengers 60+. They board before general passengers — this is not at the airline's discretion. Air India offers ~50% fare discount on base fare for passengers 60+. Free wheelchair on request. Document required: original government-issued ID with date of birth (Aadhaar, passport, voter ID).
💡 Senior Citizen Tips
→Book senior citizen fare directly on airline's website or call centre — most third-party sites don't display it.
→Request wheelchair even if you don't need it fully — gives priority access through security queues too.
→Carry all medicines in hand baggage with prescriptions — never in checked baggage.
Legal basis: DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part I; Ministry of Civil Aviation Senior Citizen Policy
VISUALLY IMPAIRED
Rights of Visually Impaired Passengers
Full escort from airport entry to aircraft seat — mandatory. Priority boarding — always, no airline can refuse. Guide dog in cabin — free on Air India (IndiGo requires advance approval). Braille or audio safety briefing must be provided. Air India offers fare concession — book at city ticketing office with disability certificate.
💬 At the Airport Counter
→"I am visually impaired and require full escort assistance from airport entry to my aircraft seat. This is mandatory under DGCA accessibility regulations."
→Call the airline 48 hours before to confirm special assistance is arranged at BOTH departure and arrival airports.
Legal basis: DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part I; Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016; Constitution Article 21

Escalation Path & Helplines
AirSewa → Nodal Officer → Consumer Forum → Court
▼
ESCALATION ORDER
1
Airline Customer Care — always first. Get complaint reference number.
2
Airline Nodal Officer — every airline must have one per DGCA mandate. Email directly.
3
AirSewa (airsewa.gov.in) — DGCA's official system. File within 30 days. Airlines respond quickly or face DGCA action.
4
District Consumer Commission — No lawyer needed up to ₹50 lakhs. File at consumerhelpline.gov.in.
5
Civil Court — for large international claims under Montreal Convention.
