Exam Answer Notes · LL.B. Semester V

📖 Story notes NEW

New: a different way to learn the same syllabus: each topic is told through its leading case. You read the facts, meet the law the court used, see how it ended, and then turn it into Part A, B and C answers. Stories stay in memory; the law comes with them.

Part AThe gold-edged lines in each chapter
Part BThe headings, with the case story as your illustration
Part CThe workshop at the end of each cluster: Issue → Law → Apply → Conclusion
