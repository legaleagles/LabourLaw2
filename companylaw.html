Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Salomon\'s separate legal entity, judicial & statutory grounds for lifting the veil, Memorandum vs Articles, Section 166 directors\' duties, Section 241 oppression grounds, Section 244 thresholds, just & equitable winding up — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Company Law exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Salomon v Salomon & Co Ltd (1897 — foundational separate legal entity doctrine) and Needle Industries v Needle Industries Newey (1981 — directors\' fiduciary duty to all members). Also know Vodafone v Union of India (2012, refused to lift the veil) and Shanti Prasad Jain v Kalinga Tubes (1965, oppression test).
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Companies Act 2013 Sections: 2(20) definition, 7 (certificate of incorporation), 9 (effect of registration), 166 (directors\' duties), 241/242/244 (oppression, powers, thresholds), 339/447 (fraud provisions). IBC 2016 reshaped winding-up.
