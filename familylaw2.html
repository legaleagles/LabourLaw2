Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Sahih/Fasid/Batil marriage classification, Prompt vs Deferred Dower, Talaq-e-Ahsan/Hasan/Biddat comparison, Section 144 BNSS maintenance rights, One-third Wasiyat limit, Hizanat vs Wilayat — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Family Law-II exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Shayara Bano v Union of India (2017 — triple talaq unconstitutional, 3:2 majority) and Mohd. Ahmed Khan v Shah Bano Begum (1985 — secular maintenance under Section 125 CrPC). Also know Danial Latifi v Union of India (2001) harmonising the 1986 Act with Shah Bano.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
1937 Shariat Application Act, 1939 Dissolution Act, 1986 Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Divorce) Act, 2017 Shayara Bano, 2019 Criminalisation Act. Iddat: 3 months (divorce) / 4 months 10 days (widow). One-third bequest limit to non-heirs.
