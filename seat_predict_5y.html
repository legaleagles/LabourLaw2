How this works: Based on your category, gender, EWS status, region (OU/Unreserved), and special category, we determine all the "Allotted Category" seat buckets you are eligible to compete in (Open Merit + EWS if applicable + your reservation category seats + special category seats if applicable), then compare your rank against the actual 2025 Round-2 closing rank (the highest among your eligible buckets) for each college.

Classification: If your rank is higher than the college's closing rank, it's not shown. Otherwise — High Chance if your rank is ≤ 60% of the closing rank, Moderate if ≤ 90%, and Less Chance otherwise.

Note on 5-Year LLB data: The 5-Year (integrated) LLB rank ranges are much smaller than 3-Year LLB — the highest rank that got any seat in 2025 was under 4,900. Some colleges (PG College of Law OU, University College of Law KU) had a separate "Selection" quota in addition to regular counselling seats, which is not included in this tool. A few category combinations (like certain BC/SC sub-group + region combinations) may not have had any 2025 seat data and won't show results — this means there's no reference data for that specific combination, not necessarily that there's no chance.

Important: Seat matrices, reservation rules, and competition levels change every year. This tool is for guidance only — always verify with the official TG LAWCET 2026 counselling website before finalizing college options.
