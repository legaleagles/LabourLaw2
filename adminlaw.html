Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Functional separation of powers, essential legislative functions, substantive vs procedural ultra vires, the two rules of natural justice, post-decisional hearing, threefold judicial review grounds, the five writs — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Administrative Law exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
In Re Delhi Laws Act (1951, 7-judge bench — essential legislative functions cannot be delegated) and Maneka Gandhi v Union of India (1978 — post-decisional hearing doctrine). Also know Ram Jawaya Kapur v State of Punjab (1955) on functional separation of powers.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
In Re Delhi Laws Act was the FIRST Presidential Reference under Article 143. Administrative Tribunals Act 1985 (CAT). Article 299 (government contract formalities). Threefold review: Illegality + Irrationality + Procedural Impropriety (Lord Diplock, GCHQ).
