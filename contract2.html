Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Co-extensive liability of surety, Particular vs General Lien, Delegatus non potest delegare, Mutual Agency test for Partnership, Condition vs Warranty, Nemo Dat Quod Non Habet exceptions — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Contract-II exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Lallan Prasad v Rahmat Ali (1967 — pawnee cannot double-recover) and Shaw & Co v Symmons & Sons (1917 — bailee\'s particular lien). These illustrate the core pledge and bailment principles most frequently tested.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Sections 124-125 (Indemnity), 126-128 (Guarantee), 148 (Bailment), 170/171 (Lien types), 172-176 (Pledge), 182 (Agency), 201 (Termination). Partnership Act S.4 (mutual agency), S.69 (unregistered firm disability). Sale of Goods Act S.4, S.12-17, S.45-54.
