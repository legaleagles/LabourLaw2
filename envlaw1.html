Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Article 21 as the right to a clean environment, Precautionary Principle & Polluter Pays Principle, Sustainable Development, Public Trust Doctrine, Article 48-A vs 51-A(g), the Environment (Protection) Act 1986 — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Environmental Law exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
M.C. Mehta v Union of India (Ganga Pollution Case, 1988 — Article 21 + Polluter Pays) and Vellore Citizens\' Welfare Forum v Union of India (1996 — Precautionary Principle + Polluter Pays + Sustainable Development). These two cover the vast majority of case-based questions in this subject.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Water Act 1974, Air Act 1981, Environment (Protection) Act 1986 (triggered by Bhopal Gas Tragedy 1984), Biological Diversity Act 2002, NGT Act 2010. Stockholm Declaration 1972 → Rio/CBD 1992 → Kyoto 1997 → Paris/COP21 2015.
