⚖️ Your Legal Rights — Solar in India
1
Net Metering is a Legal Right — not a favour. Under the Electricity Act 2003 (Sec 42) and CERC/SERC Regulations, your DISCOM is legally obligated to provide net metering within 30 days of your application. They cannot refuse without written reasons.
2
PM Surya Ghar Subsidy is an Entitlement. Eligible residential consumers have a legal right to apply. The scheme mandates processing within 30 days of application. Delay or rejection without reason is actionable through State Grievance Redressal.
3
DISCOM cannot charge you for the net meter. Under most state regulations, the cost of the net meter is borne by the DISCOM. If your vendor or DISCOM is asking you to pay for it — question it with written reference to state SERC regulations.
4
Vendor must give you a written warranty. Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) mandates that solar panels must have a minimum 10-year product warranty and 25-year performance warranty. Get this in writing — verbal warranty is not enforceable.
5
AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) must be written. Any oral AMC promise has no legal value. Insist on a signed agreement specifying: duration, what is covered, response time, penalty for delay, escalation mechanism.
6
Consumer Forum jurisdiction applies. If the vendor fails to deliver what was promised — District Consumer Commission (formerly District Forum) has jurisdiction. Complaints can be filed online at consumerhelpline.gov.in. No lawyer needed for claims up to ₹50 lakhs.
7
Export tariff must be defined in writing. The rate at which your DISCOM will credit exported units (net metering rate) must be specified in your net metering agreement. Do not proceed without this in writing.
If DISCOM Refuses or Delays
🚨 DISCOM Not Cooperating? Step-by-Step Legal Remedies
Send written application to DISCOM with acknowledgment copy. Note the date — 30-day clock starts here.
If no response in 30 days — file written complaint with State Electricity Regulatory Commission (SERC): TSERC for Telangana, APERC for AP.
Simultaneously file on National Portal: solarrooftop.gov.in — PM Surya Ghar grievance redressal is tracked here.
File Consumer Complaint at District Consumer Commission under Consumer Protection Act 2019 for deficiency of service.
If subsidy is delayed beyond 30 days without reason — SERC grievance + National Consumer Helpline: 1800-11-4000.

Has the vendor given you the agreement in writing (not just verbal)?
Verbal promises have zero legal value. Insist on a signed agreement before paying even an advance.
✓

Does the agreement mention 25-year panel performance warranty?
BIS standards mandate 25-year performance warranty (minimum 80% output at 25 years). If not in agreement — demand it in writing.
✓
LEGAL
Is the Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) scope clearly defined?
Check: What is covered? What is excluded? What is the response time? What happens if inverter fails?
✓
LEGAL
Is there a penalty clause if installation is delayed beyond agreed date?
Without a penalty clause, you have no leverage if the vendor delays. Negotiate at least ₹500/day penalty for delay.
✓
LEGAL
Is the vendor MNRE empanelled / registered?
Only MNRE empanelled vendors can facilitate PM Surya Ghar subsidy. Ask for their vendor ID and verify on pmsuryaghar.gov.in before paying.
✓
LEGAL
Does the agreement mention who will apply for net metering — vendor or you?
Many disputes arise because vendors say "you apply" but don't guide you. Insist it is vendor's responsibility to apply and obtain net meter connection.
💰 Financial Clarity
✓
FINANCIAL
Is the total cost breakdown itemised — panels, inverter, structure, wiring, installation separately?
A lump sum quote hides the quality of individual components. Ask for itemised quote — this also helps you compare vendors properly.
✓
FINANCIAL
Have you verified the subsidy amount yourself on pmsuryaghar.gov.in?
Some vendors inflate the "original" cost knowing you expect a subsidy. Calculate your subsidy independently — it is ₹30,000/kWp for first 2 kWp, ₹18,000/kWp for next 1 kWp, max ₹78,000 for 3 kWp system.
✓
FINANCIAL
Is GST clearly shown in the quote?
Solar panels attract 12% GST. If vendor says "GST extra," ensure the final payable amount including GST is what you're comparing. Hidden GST is a common trick.
✓
FINANCIAL
Is the payment schedule tied to milestones (not advance-heavy)?
Standard: 30% advance, 60% on delivery of material, 10% on commissioning. Avoid paying more than 40% before material delivery — you have no leverage if vendor disappears.
✓
FINANCIAL
Have you calculated your own break-even period using our calculator?
Never rely on the vendor's break-even calculation — they always show optimistic numbers. Use our calculator above with your own electricity tariff and consumption.
✓
FINANCIAL
Does the agreement clarify who pays for DISCOM inspection/net meter charges?
DISCOM net meter should typically be provided at no extra cost to you under state SERC regulations. If vendor is charging you for it — verify with DISCOM directly.
🔧 Technical Standards
✓
TECHNICAL
Are the panels BIS certified (IS 14286 for crystalline silicon)?
BIS certification is mandatory for subsidy eligibility. Non-BIS panels will disqualify your subsidy application. Ask for the BIS registration number.
✓
TECHNICAL
Is the inverter MNRE approved and BIS certified?
The inverter is the most failure-prone component. Insist on MNRE-approved brands. Ask for model number and verify. Common reliable brands: Growatt, SolarEdge, Fronius, Sungrow, Huawei, Delta.
✓
TECHNICAL
Has a site survey been done to check for shading, roof strength, and orientation?
Shading from a single tree or water tank can reduce output by 20-30%. A good vendor will do a shadow analysis. If they quote without visiting — be cautious.
✓
TECHNICAL
Is the mounting structure galvanised / hot-dip galvanised steel or aluminium?
Cheap painted MS structures rust within 3-4 years in Telangana/AP humidity. Insist on hot-dip galvanised steel or aluminium structure with a warranty.
✓
TECHNICAL
Will you get monitoring app access to track daily generation?
Most modern inverters come with monitoring apps. Without monitoring, you won't know if your system is underperforming. Ask specifically for this before finalising.
✓
TECHNICAL
Is the generation estimate given in writing with stated assumptions?
If vendor says "you will generate 20 units/day" — get it in writing with stated assumptions (panel wattage, peak sun hours used, efficiency). Then if actual is far lower, you have grounds for complaint.
🤝 Vendor Credibility
✓
VENDOR
Have you seen at least 3 completed installations by this vendor?
Ask for references of completed installations in your area. Visit if possible. Check how the system looks after 1-2 years — is wiring neat, structure stable?
✓
VENDOR
Has the vendor facilitated subsidy for previous customers successfully?
Ask specifically — "how many of your customers received PM Surya Ghar subsidy?" If they haven't done it before, your application may face complications.
✓
VENDOR
Have you got quotes from at least 2 other vendors for comparison?
Never buy from the first vendor. Even if you trust them, a competing quote gives you negotiation leverage and confirms the price is fair.
✓
VENDOR
Is the vendor's GST registration and company registration verifiable?
Ask for GSTIN and verify on gst.gov.in. This also ensures you get a proper GST invoice — which is required for subsidy application.
💡 How to use this section
These are negotiation strategies and red flags — both for customers to protect themselves, and for vendors to understand what informed customers will ask. Knowledge on both sides leads to better deals and fewer disputes.
Negotiation Strategies
💰
Get the Real Post-Subsidy Price First
▼
Calculate subsidy yourself before talking price. Use our calculator. Then ask vendor: "What is your price after subsidy?" Many inflate the pre-subsidy price knowing the subsidy will be deducted.
Ask: "If I don't get the subsidy, what happens?" Good vendors say "we handle the subsidy process and guarantee it or compensate." Bad vendors say "subsidy is your problem."
The effective price you should be comparing across vendors is: Total Cost − Subsidy − Any State Incentives. Not the sticker price.
⚖️ Legal angle: The subsidy amount is fixed by Government — it is the same regardless of vendor. Any vendor who says "our subsidy is higher" is misleading you.
📋
Compare Apples to Apples — Not Quotes to Quotes
▼
A ₹1,50,000 quote with Tier-1 panels and 5-year AMC is better than a ₹1,20,000 quote with no-name panels and no AMC. Always compare: panel brand + wattage per panel + inverter brand + structure material + warranty terms + AMC years.
Ask for the datasheet of the panel being offered. Check: Wattage (e.g., 545Wp per panel), Efficiency %, Temperature coefficient (how much output drops in heat — important in Hyderabad summers).
Create a simple table: Vendor A vs Vendor B vs Vendor C. Fill in: system size, brand, warranty, AMC, total cost, net cost, who handles net meter.
🚨 Red flag: Vendor who refuses to give itemised quote is hiding something — possibly using cheaper components than quoted.
🤝
What You Can Negotiate — and What You Cannot
▼
CAN negotiate: Panel brand/wattage upgrade, Additional year of AMC at no cost, Monitoring app access, Delay penalties, Extended inverter warranty, Payment schedule (milestone-based not time-based).
CANNOT negotiate: GST (fixed by law), Subsidy amount (fixed by Government), DISCOM net metering charges (fixed by SERC regulation), BIS certification requirement.
Best negotiation moment: After getting 3 quotes, go back to your preferred vendor with the lowest competing quote. Say: "I prefer your service but Vendor X quoted ₹X less for similar specs — can you match or add value?" This works well in solar.
⚖️ Legal angle: Vendors cannot legally quote different subsidy amounts — the amount is Government-fixed. If two vendors quote different "subsidy" amounts for same system size, one is misleading you.
⚠️
Red Flags — Walk Away If You See These
▼
Vendor asks for more than 40% advance before installation begins.
No written agreement — "verbal agreement is fine, we trust each other."
Cannot show BIS certificate or MNRE vendor registration when asked.
Generation guarantee is oral and very high — "you will save ₹8,000/month guaranteed."
Says "net metering takes 6 months" without explaining why or offering to follow up.
Quotes a system size that doesn't match your consumption — selling you more than you need.
Pressure to sign same day — "this price is only for today."
🚨 If vendor pressures you to sign immediately, always take 48 hours. A legitimate vendor will honour the quote for at least a week. Urgency is a sales tactic, not a legal obligation on your part.
📝
Questions to Ask Every Vendor
▼
"Can I see your MNRE vendor registration number?" — Verify on pmsuryaghar.gov.in.
"What panel brand and model are you providing? Can I see the datasheet?" — Check wattage, efficiency, temperature coefficient.
"Who applies for net metering — you or me? How many days does it typically take in this area?"
"How many PM Surya Ghar subsidy applications have you facilitated in the last 6 months? How many received subsidy?"
"What exactly is covered in the AMC? What is the response time if inverter fails?" — Get this in writing.
"What happens if my actual generation is 20% below your estimate?" — A confident vendor will have an answer; a dishonest one will get nervous.
"Can you give me contact of 2 customers who installed with you in the last year?" — Then actually call them.
⚖️ A vendor who cannot answer these questions clearly and in writing is not ready for your business. Your money, your right to full information.
📱
Right System Size for Your Consumption
▼
Rule of thumb: 1 kWp system generates approximately 4-5 units/day in South India (120-150 units/month). So if you consume 300 units/month — a 2-2.5 kWp system suffices.
Do NOT oversize just because the vendor says "future-proof." If you don't have the load, extra generation that's exported earns at lower export tariff than what you pay for consumption.
Calculate right size: Monthly consumption (units) ÷ 130 = kWp needed (approximate). Then size up by 10-15% for degradation and losses.
For commercial: Account for peak demand, sanctioned load, and DISCOM regulations on max system size relative to sanctioned load.
🚨 Common sales tactic: "Your bill will be zero with 5 kWp." If your consumption doesn't justify 5 kWp, you're being oversold. Right-size your system first.
