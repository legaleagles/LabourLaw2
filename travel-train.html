🚂 Train Passenger Rights
Railways Act 1989 · Legally Enforceable Rights
Train Passenger Rights India
Indian Railways carries 8 billion passengers a year. Your rights are not just moral expectations — they are statutory entitlements under the Railways Act 1989 and its Rules.
📋 Railways Act 1989 ⚖️ Railway Claims Tribunal 📞 RailMadad: 139
🎫
Ticket Refund & Cancellation Rights
Train cancelled · 3+ hr delay · TDR filing · Refund timelines
▼
ENTITLEMENT
Full Refund When Train is Cancelled or Delayed 3+ Hours
You are entitled to a 100% full refund — no cancellation charges — in these situations: train cancelled by Indian Railways · train delayed 3+ hours at your boarding station · you are made to travel in a lower class than booked · AC fails in AC coach (refund of AC charge difference) · train diverted and you don't wish to travel.
HOW TO CLAIM REFUND
1
For IRCTC online bookings — file TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) online at irctc.co.in before train departs from your boarding station.
2
For counter bookings — go to Reservation Office at station and surrender ticket for refund before departure.
3
Refund credited to original payment source within 5–7 working days for online bookings.
4
If denied — file claim before Railway Claims Tribunal (RCT).
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Section 52 — Cancellation of ticket and refund; Railway Passengers (Cancellation of Ticket & Refund of Fare) Rules 2015
ENTITLEMENT
Right to Travel in Lower Class — Refund of Difference
If no accommodation is available in the class you booked and you are made to travel in a lower class — you have the right to a refund of the fare difference between the class booked and the class you actually travelled in. Surrender your ticket to TTE and get a certificate.
💬 What to Do
→Immediately inform the TTE (Ticket Examiner) and demand a written certificate of the class downgrade.
→File for refund of difference at the destination station's reservation office or through IRCTC.
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Section 51 — Provision for case where ticket issued for class without accommodation
IMPORTANT RULE
TDR Must Be Filed BEFORE Departure for Full Refund
For online bookings, the TDR must be filed before the actual departure of the train from your boarding station. If you file after departure — no refund is admissible in most cases. This is the most common mistake passengers make.
⚠️ Critical Timing Rule
If your train is running 3+ hours late and you decide not to travel — file TDR online IMMEDIATELY, before the train is scheduled to depart. Don't wait to "see if it arrives." Once departed, refund is mostly gone.

Accident & Injury Compensation — Strict Liability
No need to prove negligence · Railway Claims Tribunal · 1 year to claim
▼
ENTITLEMENT — STRICT LIABILITY
Railway is Strictly Liable for Accidents — No Fault Required
If you are injured or killed in a railway accident or "untoward incident," Indian Railways is strictly liable — you do NOT need to prove negligence. Untoward incidents include: train accidents · accidental falls from running trains · electrocution on railway premises · injury while boarding or alighting. The Railway cannot use passenger's contributory negligence as a complete defence (except for suicide or self-injury).
Fixed Compensation as per Schedule
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Sections 124 & 124A — Strict liability for accidents; Railway Accidents and Untoward Incidents (Compensation) Rules 1990
Key cases: Union of India v. Prabhakaran (SC) — falling from running train is untoward incident; Jameela v. Union of India — electrocution covered
ENTITLEMENT
Railway Claims Tribunal — Claim Process
Compensation claims for accidents must be filed before the Railway Claims Tribunal (RCT) — not civil courts. RCT has exclusive jurisdiction for accident compensation, death, bodily injury, and loss of goods/luggage. No lawyer is technically required. File within 1 year of the incident.
HOW TO CLAIM ACCIDENT COMPENSATION
1
Collect: FIR copy, doctor's certificate of injury, railway accident report, hospital records.
2
Apply for interim relief to Railway Administration immediately (Section 126, Railways Act).
3
File claim application before Railway Claims Tribunal within 1 year. Application fee is minimal.
4
RCT determines compensation based on age, income, and nature of injury per the Schedule.
5
Contact nearest RCT office — they have jurisdiction in 21 cities. Also contact Railway Protection Force (RPF) helpline: 182.
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Sections 124, 124A, 126, 127; Railway Claims Tribunal Act 1987
IMPORTANT LIMIT
Luggage Theft — Not an Untoward Incident
Theft of passenger luggage in a train is NOT considered an untoward incident under Section 123(c). No automatic compensation is payable for theft unless you can prove railway negligence. Keep valuables in your custody at all times. For booked/declared luggage — railway is strictly liable as bailee (Section 73).
⚠️ Protect Your Belongings
Never leave belongings unattended. For high-value items — declare value at time of booking and pay the fee to be entitled to higher compensation if lost. Without declaration, compensation is limited.
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Sections 100, 103 — Liability for luggage; SC judgment in Union of India v. Savita Sharma

Food, Water & Hygiene Rights
Overcharging illegal · Quality standards · Coach cleanliness
▼
ENTITLEMENT
Right to Safe Food at Standard Rates
On trains with catering facilities, you have the right to hygienic food and clean drinking water. Pantry vendors must sell food at published IRCTC rates — overcharging is illegal. You have the right to see the rate card. Safe drinking water must be available at all major railway stations free of charge.
💬 If Overcharged
→Demand the printed rate card. All pantry staff must display it. Photograph overcharge evidence.
→File complaint on RailMadad app (helpline 139) with your PNR and coach number.
→For food quality issues — SMS your PNR to 139. Railway must respond.
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Sections 57 & 57A; Food Safety and Standards Act 2006; Consumer Protection Act 2019
ENTITLEMENT
Right to a Clean Coach
Under the 'Clean My Coach' initiative, you can request cleaning by sending an SMS with your PNR to 139 or using the RailMadad app. Coach cleaning staff must respond. Repeated unresponsiveness is a consumer complaint ground.
Legal basis: Ministry of Railways Clean My Coach initiative; Consumer Protection Act 2019 — Deficiency in Service

Berth & Seat Rights
Confirmed ticket = confirmed berth · Lower class rights · No overcrowding
▼
ENTITLEMENT
Confirmed Ticket Guarantees Your Berth
A confirmed ticket guarantees a berth or seat. If the berth is not available — the Railway must provide alternate accommodation of the same or higher class, or issue a full refund. TTE cannot deny you access to your booked berth without providing an equivalent alternative.
💬 If Your Berth is Taken
→Show your ticket to TTE. Ask them to restore your berth or provide equivalent alternative immediately.
→If TTE is unhelpful — call 139 from inside the train. This is the railway helpline staffed 24/7.
→Note TTE name and badge number. File complaint on RailMadad app with your PNR, train number, and coach.
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Section 51 & 60; Ministry of Railways Circular 2016
ENTITLEMENT
Ladies' Compartment — Mandatory Earmarking
Every passenger train must have a dedicated ladies' compartment. No male passenger (except small children) can travel in a ladies' compartment. This is a statutory requirement, not a courtesy. Violation is punishable.
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Section 58 — Earmarking of compartment for ladies
ENTITLEMENT
Right Against Overcrowding
Each compartment has a legally maximum passenger count (Section 57). Overcrowding that results in injury makes Railways liable for failure of duty of care. SC has held Railways responsible for injuries caused by overcrowding (SC case on overcrowding, 2018).
Legal basis: Railways Act 1989, Section 57 — Maximum number of passengers per compartment

Premium Train Rights (Rajdhani, Shatabdi, Vande Bharat, Tejas)
Delay compensation · Meal entitlements · Insurance
▼
ENTITLEMENT
Delay Compensation on Premium Trains
Passengers on Tejas Express are entitled to compensation for delays: ₹100 for delay of 1+ hour; ₹250 for delay of 2+ hours. This is automatically credited. Passengers on Rajdhani and Shatabdi are entitled to meal refunds or free meals if delayed significantly.
Legal basis: Ministry of Railways Circular 2019 (Tejas compensation policy); IRCTC Tejas/Vande Bharat Service Standards
ENTITLEMENT
Free Travel Insurance on IRCTC Bookings
When booking through IRCTC, passengers can opt for travel insurance at ₹0.45 per passenger. This covers: death (₹10 lakh), permanent total disability (₹10 lakh), permanent partial disability (₹7.5 lakh), hospitalisation (₹2 lakh), transportation of mortal remains (₹10,000). This is completely optional but very low cost.
💡 Always Opt In
→At ₹0.45 per person, always select "Yes" for travel insurance on IRCTC. ₹10 lakh coverage for less than a rupee is exceptional value.
→Keep your PNR number — this is your insurance policy number. Claims through IRCTC insurance partner.
