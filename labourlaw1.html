Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Bangalore Water Supply's "industry" test, Lay-Off vs Retrenchment, Section 25F conditions, the dispute-settlement ladder, trade union registration, Standing Orders thresholds -- and current updates under the Industrial Relations Code 2020 (300-worker threshold, negotiating unions) -- appear almost every year in OU LLB Labour Law-I exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Bangalore Water Supply v A. Rajappa (1978, 7-judge bench -- expansive "industry" definition) and State Bank of India v N. Sundara Money (1976 -- non-renewal as retrenchment, later partly reversed by the 1984 Amendment). Also know Hospital Mazdoor Sabha on hospitals as "industry."
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
IR Code 2020 in force 21 Nov 2025. ID Act threshold 100 workers RAISED to 300 under IR Code (lay-off/retrenchment/closure permission AND Standing Orders). Section 25F: 1-month notice + 15 days' pay/year + govt notice. Trade union registration: 7+ members. Negotiating union: 51%+; council: 20%+ per union.
