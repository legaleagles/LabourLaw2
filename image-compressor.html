How it works
This re-encodes your photo at a lower quality setting, which reduces file size with a small, usually unnoticeable, drop in visual quality. Higher quality = larger file, smaller file = more visible compression. Try "Balanced" first — it's a good middle ground for sharing photos where you don't need print-quality detail.
