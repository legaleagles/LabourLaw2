Exam Strategy

⚡ AIBE Weightage Fact
Constitutional Law contributes 20-25% of the entire AIBE paper (typically ~10 direct questions) -- the single highest-weightage subject in the exam. Questions are direct, factual, and heavily repeated year over year -- exact Article numbers and Amendment years score marks, not conceptual essays.
🏛️ Most-Repeated Case Sequence
Shankari Prasad (1951) -> Golaknath (1967) -> Kesavananda Bharati (1973) -> Minerva Mills (1980) is one of the single most repeated AIBE question patterns across multiple past papers. Know all four, in order, with exact years and one-line holdings.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
42nd Amendment 1976 (Preamble words + Fundamental Duties). 44th Amendment 1978 (armed rebellion). 86th Amendment 2002 (Art 21A). President min age 35, term 5 years. SC judges retire at 65, HC judges at 62. Article 22: 24-hour production before Magistrate.
