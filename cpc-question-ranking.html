How to use this ranking

1Part A first. It repeats the most — the top 10 topics cover about one in three short notes ever asked. Prepare every topic asked in 4 or more papers.
2Part B — four big essays. Res judicata, the plaint, general principles of limitation and modes of execution stand well above the rest. Write each once in full.
3Part C — learn the test, not the story. Most problems appear once with new facts. Learn the rule behind the repeaters: adverse possession, S.80 notice, place of suing, ex parte remedies, arrest before judgment.
4Watch the "asked since 2022" tag. A topic that is both frequent and recent deserves the most attention.
