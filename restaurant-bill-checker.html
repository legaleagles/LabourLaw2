Step 2 · Verify

The 60-Second Bill Checklist

Run through these four checks every time the bill arrives.

🧾
Check every line item
  • Match items and quantities against what you actually ordered
  • Watch for billed "complimentary" items — papad, chutney, water
  • Question any "cover charge" you weren't told about upfront
⚠️
Verify the GST
  • Standalone restaurant / dhaba / café → GST must be 5%
  • 18% is valid only in hotels with room tariff above ₹7,500/night
  • A valid 15-digit GSTIN must be printed — no GSTIN means they cannot legally collect GST at all
  • GST must never be calculated on top of the service charge
🙅
Service charge — your call, not theirs
  • 5–10% "service charge" is NOT a government tax — it is optional
  • Politely say: "Please remove the service charge" — they must comply
  • If you keep it, don't tip separately — that's paying twice
  • If they refuse: note it, keep the bill, complain on 1915
💳
Pay the smart way
  • Before paying, check dining apps for instant pay-bill discounts (15–30%)
  • Check your credit card's dining offers — many run weekday tie-ups
  • Ask staff directly about running offers — they rarely volunteer them
  • Tip on the pre-tax food amount, not the grand total
Your Legal Backing
📜
CCPA Guidelines, 4 July 2022
No hotel or restaurant shall add service charge automatically or by default, and it cannot be made a condition of entry or service.
⚖️
Delhi High Court, 2025
Upheld the CCPA guidelines — mandatory service charge is an unfair trade practice. Paying it is the customer's choice.
🛡️
Consumer Protection Act, 2019
Overcharging, fake taxes, and forced charges are unfair trade practices. You can complain and claim a refund.
