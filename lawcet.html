Document Checklist
Keep these ready before you start registration
▼
REQUIRED FOR EVERYONE
Core Documents
  • TS LAWCET Rank Card / Hall Ticket
  • Aadhaar Card (or other valid photo ID)
  • SSC (10th) marks memo / certificate
  • Intermediate (12th) marks memo — for 5-year LLB
  • Graduation marks memo — for 3-year LLB
  • Provisional or Degree Certificate of your qualifying exam
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Study certificates from Class 1 (or 5th) up to your qualifying exam
  • Recent passport-size photographs
IF APPLICABLE TO YOU
Category & Special Documents
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/BC categories)
  • Income Certificate (for fee reimbursement / EWS)
  • Local/Residence Certificate (Telangana candidates)
  • Migration Certificate (if you studied outside Telangana)
  • NCC / CAP / Sports / PwD certificate, issued by the competent authority
  • Employer Certificate — if a parent works for State/Central Govt, PSU, or similar institutions
⚠️ Common Mistake
Category and special-certificate candidates often need PHYSICAL, in-person verification at a designated centre — not just an upload. Always check whether your category requires slot booking, and don't leave this to the last day.

Seat Reservation Rules — Updated 2025
85% Local (O.U. Area) + 15% Unreserved — per G.O.Ms.No.15
▼
RULE CHANGE, DATED 27.02.2025
Region-wise Reservation Was Replaced
Telangana's 10-year Article 371-D protection period (from the 2014 Andhra Pradesh Reorganization Act) ended in 2024. G.O.Ms.No.15, Higher Education (TE) Department, dated 27.02.2025, replaced the earlier region-wise reservation system with a new formula that still governs TG LAWCET admissions today.
THE NEW FORMULA
●85% of seats in each course are reserved for LOCAL CANDIDATES of the O.U. (Osmania University) Area, as defined under the Andhra Pradesh Educational Institutions (Regulations and Admissions) Order, 1974.
●15% of seats are UNRESERVED, open to four categories of candidates (see below).
WHO CAN COMPETE FOR THE 15% UNRESERVED SEATS
Four Eligible Categories
  • All candidates already eligible to be declared local candidates of O.U. Area
  • Candidates who have resided in the State for a total of 10 years (excluding periods of study outside the State) — OR whose parent has resided in the State for 10 years (excluding periods of employment outside the State)
  • Candidates who are children of parents employed by the State/Central Government, PSUs, Local Bodies, Universities, or similar quasi-government institutions within the State
  • Candidates who are spouses of persons employed by the State/Central Government, PSUs, Local Bodies, Universities, or recognised educational institutions within the State
⚠️ What This Means For You
If you are NOT a local candidate of O.U. Area, you can only compete for the 15% unreserved seats — and only if you meet one of the four categories above. This is a genuinely significant change from the old region-wise system; if your understanding of "local status" predates 2025, it is likely outdated.

The Counselling Process, Step by Step
Registration → Verification → Web Options → Allotment → Reporting
▼
THE FULL SEQUENCE
1
Online Registration — log in with your Hall Ticket Number and Rank at the official counselling portal, pay the registration fee, and fill your personal/academic details carefully.
2
Upload & Verify Documents — upload scanned copies of every document above. Category/special candidates may additionally need physical verification at a designated centre.
3
Check the Verified List — once published, confirm your name appears correctly. If there's an error in your details, raise it immediately — don't wait.
4
Web Options (Choice Filling) — rank your preferred colleges and courses in order. This is the single most important step — see our tips below before you fill this.
5
Freeze Your Options — once frozen, you generally cannot edit them again for that round. Only freeze when you're genuinely certain.
6
Seat Allotment — check the allotment result online using your login credentials.
7
Report to Your Allotted College — with ALL original certificates and your acknowledgement/allotment letter, within the window given. Missing this deadline can forfeit your seat.

Web-Options & College Selection Tips
The decisions that actually matter
▼
BEFORE YOU FILL OPTIONS
●Fill MORE options than you think you need. An option you rank last still protects you if higher choices don't come through — it costs nothing to add it.
●Order options by genuine preference, not just "safe" choices — your true first choice should always be ranked first, since a higher-preference seat is offered to you first if you qualify for it.
●Research each college's location, fee structure, and placement record before ranking it — don't rank colleges you haven't actually looked into.
●Use our rank/college prediction tool (linked from our YouTube channel above) as one input — but cross-check it against the official cutoff data TGCHE publishes each round.
●Take a screenshot or printout of your final frozen options as proof, in case of any portal discrepancy later.

Frequently Asked Questions
Real concerns, answered in plain language
▼
Am I a "local" candidate or "non-local" now?
Since G.O.Ms.No.15 (2025), the old region-wise system is gone. What matters now is whether you qualify as a local candidate of O.U. Area under the 1974 Order. If you don't, you can still compete — but only for the 15% unreserved seats, and only if you meet one of the four categories listed in the Reservation Rules section above. When in doubt, check with your Help Line Centre during document verification.
What happens if I miss a counselling deadline?
Missing Phase 1 doesn't end your chances. Candidates who didn't participate in Phase 1, didn't get a seat, or were allotted a seat but didn't report, are all eligible for Phase 2. You will need to register and exercise web options fresh for Phase 2, even if you already did so in Phase 1.
Can I change my web options after I freeze them?
Not after the freeze deadline — this is one of the most common regrets candidates have. There is usually a short "option editing" window before the final freeze where you can still add, remove, or reorder colleges. Use that window carefully; once truly frozen, no further changes are allowed for that round.
Is the counselling registration fee refundable?
No. The counselling registration/processing fee is non-refundable under all circumstances, regardless of whether you get a seat. Separately, TUITION FEE cancellation works differently: cancelling before the cutoff date in your allotment order typically gets you a 50% refund; cancelling after that date generally gets no refund at all.
Do I need to visit anywhere in person, or is it all online?
Most of the process — registration, document upload, web options — is fully online. The main EXCEPTION is special category certificates (NCC, CAP, Sports, PwD), which usually require physical, in-person verification at a designated centre by slot booking. Regular candidates typically only need to physically report once, to their allotted college, after seat allotment.
What if there's an error in my verified details?
Raise it immediately, before seat allotment is declared. Corrections to the verified candidate list are generally NOT entertained once allotment has been announced — this is a hard cutoff, so don't assume you can fix a mistake later.
3-year LLB vs 5-year LLB — do my documents differ?
Mostly the same checklist, with one key difference: 5-year LLB candidates submit their Intermediate (12th) marks memo as the qualifying exam certificate, while 3-year LLB candidates (who are already graduates) submit their Graduation marks memo and degree certificate instead.
I got a seat in Phase 1 but want a different college — what now?
You're allowed to participate in Phase 2 to try for a better option. Note that doing this means locking a completely fresh set of web options for Phase 2 — your Phase 1 choices don't automatically carry over.
Does our rank/college prediction tool guarantee my seat?
No prediction tool can guarantee an outcome — seat allotment depends on your final rank, the seats actually available that round, other candidates' choices, and the reservation category rules. Treat any predictor (ours included) as a planning aid, not a promise, and always cross-check against TGCHE's own official cutoff data as each round is announced.
