Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Facts in Issue vs Relevant Facts, Admissions vs Confessions, Dying Declaration essentials, May/Shall Presume vs Conclusive Proof, Examination-in-Chief/Cross/Re-Examination sequence, Primary vs Secondary Evidence — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Law of Evidence exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Khushal Rao v State of Bombay (1958 — dying declaration alone can sustain conviction, no absolute corroboration rule) and Anvar P.V v P.K. Basheer (2014 — electronic evidence needs statutory certification). Also know Laxman v State of Maharashtra on medical certification.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
BSA effective 1 July 2024, Act No. 47 of 2023. Section 4 (facts in issue/relevant facts only), Section 23 (discovery exception), Section 25 (admissions not conclusive), Section 26(a) (dying declaration). 30 years = document genuineness presumption threshold.
