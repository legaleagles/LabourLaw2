Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Mitakshara vs Dayabhaga, Coparcenary & the 2005 Amendment (daughters as coparceners), Karta's powers of alienation, Conditions for valid marriage (Section 5), Restitution of Conjugal Rights (Section 9), Succession under Sections 8 & 15 — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Family Law-I exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Vineeta Sharma v Rakesh Sharma (daughters as coparceners by birth, retrospective), Saroj Rani v Sudarshan Kumar Chopra (Section 9 constitutionality upheld), Chanmuniya v Virendra Kumar Singh Kushwaha (ceremonial requirements mandatory). These three cover most case-based questions in this subject.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Section 5 = marriage conditions (age 21/18). Section 9 = Restitution. Section 11 = Void marriages. Section 12 = Voidable marriages. Section 13 = Divorce grounds. Section 6 (Hindu Succession Act, 2005 amendment) = daughters as coparceners. 9 September 2005 = amendment commencement date.
