One row per device (a random id kept in that browser), newest first. Place and network come from the IP address, so they show the internet provider's location, which can be a nearby city rather than where the person is. Phone networks such as Jio and Airtel share one IP among many users, so an IP alone doesn't identify a person. Visits are kept in Google Cloud Logging for 30 days. Only visits after this log was switched on appear here.
