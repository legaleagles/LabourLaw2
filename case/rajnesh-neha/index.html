6Rajnesh v. Neha

(2021) 2 SCC 324
Supreme Court of India (Indu Malhotra J)
Maintenance · guidelines for amount and overlap

In 30 seconds

Story: Conflicting maintenance orders under different laws delayed relief to a wife and child.

Held: The Court laid down guidelines: affidavits of income, factors for the amount, adjustment of overlapping orders, payment from the date of application.

🧠 Disclose, decide, pay from the start

The story

A wife and son were awarded interim maintenance; the husband did not pay and challenged the orders. The Court used the case to settle the confusion caused by parallel claims under S.125 CrPC, HMA, HAMA and the DV Act.

The question

How should courts decide maintenance and avoid conflicting orders?

What the court held

Both parties must file affidavits disclosing assets and liabilities. The amount depends on status, reasonable needs, income and liabilities of both, the wife's own income, and children's needs. Maintenance under one law must be adjusted against another. It is payable from the date of the application.

Principles to quote

  1. S.24 and S.25 HMA; S.18 HAMA; S.144 BNSS (S.125 CrPC).
  2. An earning wife can still claim if her income is insufficient to maintain her standard of living.
