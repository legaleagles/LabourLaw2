1Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar

(1991) 1 SCC 598 · AIR 1991 SC 420
Supreme Court of India
Right to pollution-free water and air under Article 21

In 30 seconds

Story: A petitioner complained that a company's sludge was polluting the Bokaro river — but he had a personal grudge.

Held: Article 21 includes the right to enjoy pollution-free water and air; but a PIL for private motives is not entertained.

🧠 Clean air and water are life

The story

Subhash Kumar filed a PIL alleging that Tata Iron and Steel Co. was discharging sludge into the Bokaro river, polluting it. It emerged that he had been buying the slurry and had a business dispute with the company.

The question

Does Article 21 include a right to a pollution-free environment, and can a PIL be filed for private interest?

What the court held

The right to life under Article 21 includes the right to enjoyment of pollution-free water and air for full enjoyment of life; a citizen may move the Court under Article 32 to remove pollution. But PIL cannot be used for personal gain; the petition was dismissed with costs because it was motivated by self-interest.

Principles to quote

  1. Right to wholesome environment is part of Art. 21.
  2. PIL must be bona fide.
