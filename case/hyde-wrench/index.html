4Hyde v. Wrench

(1840) 3 Beav 334
Court of Chancery, England
Counter-offer destroys the original offer

In 30 seconds

Story: Offered a farm for £1,000, the buyer offered £950; when refused, he tried to accept £1,000.

Held: No contract: a counter-offer rejects the original offer, which cannot be revived.

🧠 A counter-offer kills the first offer

The story

Wrench offered to sell his farm to Hyde for £1,000. Hyde replied offering £950.

Wrench refused. Hyde then wrote that he would pay £1,000 after all, and when Wrench would not sell, Hyde sued for specific performance.

The question

Could Hyde accept the original offer after making a counter-offer?

What the court held

No. Hyde's offer of £950 was a counter-offer that rejected the original offer. Once rejected, the original offer was dead and could not be accepted later. Hyde's £1,000 letter was only a fresh offer, which Wrench was free to refuse.

Principles to quote

  1. Acceptance must be absolute and unqualified (S.7).
  2. A counter-offer or conditional acceptance is a rejection of the offer.
  3. A rejected offer lapses (S.6) and cannot be revived by the offeree.
