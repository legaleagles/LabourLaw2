5Narmada Bachao Andolan v. Union of India

(2000) 10 SCC 664
Supreme Court of India (B.N. Kirpal J)
Large dams · environmental clearance · rehabilitation

In 30 seconds

Story: An NGO challenged the Sardar Sarovar dam for displacing people and harming the environment.

Held: The dam could go ahead, step by step, with full rehabilitation and environmental safeguards; courts do not decide policy.

🧠 Develop, but rehabilitate

The story

The Narmada Bachao Andolan challenged the construction of the Sardar Sarovar dam on the Narmada river, arguing that environmental clearance was given without full studies and that lakhs of people would be displaced.

The question

Can the Court stop a large development project on environmental and displacement grounds?

What the court held

The Court allowed construction to continue in stages, linked to rehabilitation of displaced persons (pari passu) and compliance with environmental safeguards monitored by authorities. Policy decisions on large projects are for the executive; courts intervene only where there is violation of law or rights. Displacement does not by itself violate Art. 21 if adequate rehabilitation is provided.

Principles to quote

  1. Environmental clearance (EIA) is required for large projects.
  2. Balance between development and environment.

Using it in the exam

Part ARight to development; sustainable development.
Part BRight to development; judicial review of projects.
ProblemsDam without environmental clearance (Feb 2013).
