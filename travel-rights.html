Quick Facts Every Traveller Should Know
✈️
Flight delayed 2+ hrs? Free meals mandatory under DGCA. 6+ hrs? Full refund or rebooking — your choice. These are laws, not policies.
🚂
Train cancelled or 3+ hrs late? File TDR online BEFORE the train departs for full refund. After departure — no refund in most cases.
🚨
Road or bus accident? You do NOT need to prove negligence for initial compensation (MVA Sections 140–144). Golden hour hospital treatment is cashless under Section 162.
🛺
Auto refusing meter? Note registration number and file with RTA. Multiple complaints = permit cancellation. Telangana RTA: 1800-425-0022.
🧳
Baggage lost at airport? File PIR BEFORE leaving the baggage hall. After you exit — your claim weakens dramatically. Domestic max: ₹20,000.
🤝
Helping accident victim? Section 134A MVA protects Good Samaritans from civil and criminal liability. You cannot be detained for helping.
