Exam Strategy

⚡ Trainer's Top Priority
Sections 9, 10, 11, and 20 are the most repeated CPC provisions on AIBE papers, by a wide margin. Master Res Sub Judice (S.10, pending suit) vs Res Judicata (S.11, decided suit) FIRST -- this pairing alone accounts for a large share of CPC's ~10 marks.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Satyadhyan Ghosal v. Deorjin Debi (1960, res judicata across stages of same proceeding) and Srihari Hanumandas Totala v. Hemant Vithal Kamat (2021, res judicata not decidable via Order VII Rule 11). Also know Mathura Prasad v. Dossabhoi Jeejeebhoy and Indian Bank v. Satyam Fibres.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
S.9 (jurisdiction), S.10 (res sub judice), S.11 (res judicata), S.20 (place of suing), S.80 (2-month govt notice). O.VI R.17 (amendment), O.VII R.11 (plaint rejection), O.39 (injunction: 3-month detention, 1-year attachment). S.100 (Second Appeal: substantial question of law ONLY).
