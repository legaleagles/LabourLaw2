How to Ace This Exam

›
▶Every Part C problem: start with ISSUE, then LAW, then ANALYSIS, then DECISION
▶Always cite at least 2 cases per long essay — examiner looks for this
▶Mention Constitutional Articles (14, 21, 23, 39d, 41, 42, 43) wherever possible — easy marks
▶For gratuity problems — always check if reason is death/disability (5yr rule waived)
▶For ESI problems — coverage is based on wages not hours — part-timers are covered
▶Conclude every answer — even 2 lines. Examiners reward structured endings
