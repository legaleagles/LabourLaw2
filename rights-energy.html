Electricity Bill & DISCOM Rights
Dispute bill · Meter testing · No disconnection without notice
▼
ENTITLEMENT
Right to Dispute Any Electricity Bill Within 15 Days
You can dispute any electricity bill within 15 days of receipt by submitting a written objection to your DISCOM. DISCOM must respond within 7 days. Wrong meter reading, average billing without actual reading, sudden jump in consumption — all are valid grounds. You cannot be disconnected while a bill dispute is pending.
WHAT TO DO
1
Submit written bill dispute to DISCOM consumer service centre. Get acknowledgment.
2
Request meter reading verification — DISCOM must test within 7 days.
3
If meter found faulty — bill must be revised. Excess amount refunded or adjusted.
4
If DISCOM unresponsive — file complaint with State Electricity Regulatory Commission.
5
TSERC (Telangana): tserc.gov.in · APERC (AP): aperc.gov.in
⚠️ Wrongful Disconnection
DISCOM cannot disconnect without: (1) prior notice of at least 15 days, (2) proper bill with clear amount. Wrongful disconnection is a violation — file with SERC and claim compensation.
Legal basis: Electricity Act 2003, Sections 42, 56; TSERC/APERC Consumer Grievance Redressal Regulations; Consumer Protection Act 2019
ENTITLEMENT
Right to Accurate Metering & Average Billing Limits
You have the right to an accurate, tested meter. If DISCOM gives average bills (without actual reading) for more than 2 consecutive months — you can demand meter testing at DISCOM's cost. If meter is defective — bill for the period of fault must be reassessed based on previous consumption history.
💬 What to Say
→"I have not received actual meter reading for [X] months. Under Electricity Act Section 55, I demand meter testing at your cost within 7 days."
→"My bill shows [X] units this month vs [Y] units average. There is no explanation. I dispute this bill under SERC regulations."
Legal basis: Electricity Act 2003, Sections 55, 56; State DISCOM Supply Code Regulations on metering
☀️
Solar & Net Metering Rights
Right to install · Net metering mandatory · Subsidy entitlement
▼
ENTITLEMENT
Net Metering is a Legal Right — DISCOM Cannot Refuse
Under the Electricity Act 2003 and SERC Net Metering Regulations — every consumer has the legal right to install rooftop solar and get net metering connection. DISCOM must process net metering application within 30 days. They cannot charge for the net meter itself. Refusal or delay is a violation — file with SERC.
WHAT TO DO
1
Submit written net metering application to DISCOM. Keep acknowledgment.
2
If no response in 30 days — file complaint with State SERC (tserc.gov.in / aperc.gov.in).
3
DISCOM cannot charge for net meter installation — it is covered under regulations.
4
Apply for PM Surya Ghar subsidy at pmsuryaghar.gov.in after net metering approval.
5
DISCOM delay compensation: SERC can order interest on subsidy delay.
Legal basis: Electricity Act 2003, Section 42; CERC Net Metering Regulations 2021; State SERC Net Metering Regulations; PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana 2024
ENTITLEMENT
PM Surya Ghar Subsidy — Mandatory Processing in 30 Days
Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (2024) — residential consumers can get subsidy of up to ₹78,000 for 3kW system. DISCOM must process subsidy application within 30 days. Banks must provide collateral-free loans. If DISCOM delays — escalate to solarrooftop.gov.in national portal.
💬 What to Say
→1 kW system: ₹30,000 subsidy · 2 kW: ₹60,000 · 3 kW+: ₹78,000
→Must use MNRE-empanelled vendors for subsidy eligibility.
→Apply first on pmsuryaghar.gov.in — do not install before registration.
→Bank loan (PM Surya Ghar) must be collateral-free for systems up to ₹2 lakh.
Legal basis: PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana 2024; MNRE Guidelines; Electricity Act 2003; SERC Net Metering Regulations
