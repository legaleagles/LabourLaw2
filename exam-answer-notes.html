⚡ Quick revision: the night before and on the way to the exam

Don't carry the full notes to the exam centre. Quick revision squeezes every cluster into what you must recall: for each answer a 🧠 anchor sentence whose first letters give you the numbered headings, then the key words, sections, numbers, cases and Part C answers. 🔥 shows how many times a question has been asked.

  1. 📖 Read mode (night before): read one cluster at a time, say each anchor aloud and link every letter to its heading. Tap "Mark this cluster as revised"; your progress is saved on your phone.
  2. 🙈 Test me mode (active recall): the answers hide behind stripes. Say the heading or point first, then tap to check. What you miss, read again.
  3. 🦴 Skeleton mode (on the way): only anchors, headings, sections, numbers and case names: your answer plans in ten minutes a paper.
  4. No network at the centre? Download the 📱 phone PDF before you leave; it opens offline. The 🖨️ print PDF is 3 to 6 A4 pages a paper.

Turn these notes into your own audio and video lessons

Google's free NotebookLM (now called Gemini Notebook) reads a PDF and makes a podcast-style conversation between two AI hosts, or a narrated video, in English or Telugu. It's useful when you're driving, travelling or short on time.

  1. Pick one cluster. Open a book above, tap "One cluster at a time" and download the 📱 phone PDF. One cluster per notebook keeps the lesson focused.
  2. Open notebooklm.google, sign in with your Google account, create a new notebook and add the PDF as a source.
  3. Audio: in the Studio panel, choose Audio Overview and open its options before generating. Format: Deep Dive (two hosts talk it through) or Brief (about 2 minutes). Language: Telugu or English. Length: Shorter, Default or Longer (Longer is English only). Paste a prompt from below, then generate. It takes a few minutes; you can change the speed or download it.
  4. Video: in Studio, choose Video Overview, then Explainer (or Short for about a 60-second recap). Pick the language, paste the same prompt and generate. Videos can take 30 minutes or more.
  5. Ask doubts: type questions in the chat box. Answers come from your PDF, with links to the exact page.
