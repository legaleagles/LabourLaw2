How to Ace This Exam

›
▶Always cite Article 300A (not Article 31 — that was removed) for property rights questions
▶For LARR Act questions — always state the key numbers: SIA 6 months, consent 80%/70%, return after 5 years
▶Ceiling limits — memorise the 3 tiers: 54/27/10 acres for Telangana
▶Samatha case (1997) — most important case for tribal land. Must cite in Scheduled Areas questions
▶For FRA 2006 — always mention December 13, 2005 cut-off date and 3 generations / 75 years
▶Conclude every answer — even 2 lines. Examiners reward structured endings
