Advocate's Duties — Quick Reference
⚖️ Duties to Court (Chapter II, Part VI BCI Rules)
Act with dignity and self-respect
Maintain respectful attitude toward court
Never influence court by illegal means
Use only legitimate means to protect client
Appear in proper dress code
Not act in matter where personally interested
Never knowingly make false statement of fact/law
Not suppress material facts from court
👤 Duties to Client (Chapter II, Part VI BCI Rules)
Maintain fiduciary relationship — absolute trust
Never deceive client
Not charge unconscionable fees
Not withhold client's documents as lien
Maintain confidentiality of client communications
Not appear against former client in same matter
Not purchase client's property in litigation
Give honest opinion even if unfavourable
👥 Duties to Opponent & Public
Not communicate with opposite party directly
Not intimidate or obstruct witnesses
Not make false statements about opponent
Cooperate with professional colleagues
Provide free legal aid when required
Uphold rule of law and Constitution
