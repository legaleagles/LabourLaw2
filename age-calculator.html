Why "as of" matters
Many exams, jobs, and government schemes have an age cutoff calculated as of a SPECIFIC date (e.g. "must be under 30 as of August 1st"), not today's date. Use the "Calculate Age As Of" field to check your age on that exact cutoff date, not just your current age.
