Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Damnum Sine Injuria vs Injuria Sine Damno, Strict vs Absolute Liability, Assault vs Battery, Libel vs Slander, elements of Negligence, Consumer Protection Act 2019 (CCPA, Product Liability) — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Torts & Consumer Protection exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Rylands v Fletcher (Strict Liability, 1868), M.C. Mehta v Union of India (Absolute Liability, Oleum Gas Leak, 1987), and Donoghue v Stevenson (Negligence, "neighbour principle," 1932). These three cover most case-based questions in this subject.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Consumer Protection Act 2019 replaced the 1986 Act. 3-tier redressal: District/State/National Commission. Rylands v Fletcher (1868) has exceptions; M.C. Mehta (1987) Absolute Liability has NONE. Indian Medical Association v V.P. Shantha (1995) = medical services are "service" under the Act.
