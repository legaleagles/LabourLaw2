Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Offer vs Invitation to Offer, Free Consent (all 5 vitiating factors), Doctrine of Frustration (Section 56), Privity of Contract exceptions, Damages (Hadley v Baxendale) — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Contract-I exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Mohori Bibee v Dharmodas Ghose (minor's contract void ab initio), Carlill v Carbolic Smoke Ball (general offer), Balfour v Balfour (intention to contract), Chinnaya v Ramayya (consideration from third party), Hadley v Baxendale (remoteness of damages). Five cases cover most case-based questions.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
Section 10 = essentials of a valid contract. Section 11 = competence to contract. Section 15-18 = Coercion/Undue Influence/Fraud/Misrepresentation. Section 56 = Frustration. Section 74 = Liquidated damages/penalty. Section 25 = 3 exceptions to "no consideration, no contract."
