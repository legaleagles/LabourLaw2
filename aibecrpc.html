Exam Strategy

⚡ Trainer's Top Priority
Master the CrPC-to-BNSS correspondence table FIRST -- coaches rate this the single highest-yield thing you can do for this subject. A large share of repeated questions is simply "which section is this now?" between the old and new codes.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Lalita Kumari v. State of U.P. (2014, mandatory FIR) and D.K. Basu v. State of West Bengal (1997, arrest/custody safeguards). Also know Joginder Kumar, State of Rajasthan v. Balchand ("bail is the rule"), and Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI (2022).
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
FIR: 154 CrPC = 173 BNSS. Arrest: 41 = 35. Anticipatory Bail: 438 = 482. Default Bail: 167(2) = 187. BNSS came into force 1 July 2024, has 531 sections vs CrPC's 484. Section 479: first-time offenders released after 1/3 max sentence.
