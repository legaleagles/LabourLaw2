Exam Strategy

⚡ High Frequency Topics
Section 6 non-transferable interests, Vested vs Contingent, Rule Against Perpetuity formula, Section 13\'s whole-remaining-interest rule, Six kinds of mortgage, Lease vs Licence, Lis Pendens & Part Performance essentials — these appear almost every year in OU LLB Law of Property exams.
🏛️ Must-Know Cases
Bellamy v Sabine (1857 — Lis Pendens rests on necessity, not notice) and Mohd. Musa v Aghor Kumar Ganguli (1914, Privy Council — Part Performance applicable in India even before statutory codification). Also know Saradamani Kandappan v S. Rajalakshmi (2011) reaffirming Part Performance.
🔢 Numbers That Fetch Marks
TPA effective 1 July 1882. Section 6 (non-transferable interests), 10 (restraint on alienation void), 13 (unborn persons), 14 (perpetuity), 35 (election), 52 (lis pendens), 53A (part performance, added 1929), 54 (sale), 58 (mortgage, 6 kinds), 105 (lease), 122-123 (gift).
