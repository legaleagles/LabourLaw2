Real Estate Reality Check

Unapproved layouts, super-area inflation, "prohibited land," fake amenities — the biggest purchase of your life deserves 10 real minutes of checking. RERA, Bhu Bharati, and the actual law, explained simply.

🔍 Verify Before You Sign Anything
Why this matters more than any other purchase
A wrong saree costs you a few thousand rupees. A wrong property can cost you your life savings, years in court, or land you can never legally build on. Every check below is free and takes minutes — none of them require a lawyer to start.

📜
RERA Registration Number
▾
Under Section 3 of the RERA Act, any project over 500 sq.m or more than 8 apartments must be registered before it can legally be advertised or sold. Registration forces the builder to disclose land title, approvals, layout, and timelines — and to deposit 70% of buyer funds in a project-specific escrow account under Section 4, so money can't be diverted to another project.
How to check: Ask for the RERA registration number and verify it directly on rera.telangana.gov.in (or your state's RERA portal). No number, or a number that doesn't verify, means no legal protection at all — this is the single most important check on this list.
🗺️
Land Title on Bhu Bharati (Telangana)
▾
Telangana replaced the old Dharani portal with Bhu Bharati on 14 April 2025. Every land parcel now has a 14-digit Bhudhaar ID, similar to Aadhaar for land, and ownership records are viewable online. A key section — 22A — lists land that's legally prohibited from being registered at all (government land, assigned land, disputed land).
How to check: Search the survey number on bhubharati.telangana.gov.in. Confirm the seller's name matches ownership records, and specifically check the land isn't on the Section 22A prohibited list — such land legally cannot be registered to you, no matter what the seller promises.
📋
Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
▾
An EC shows every registered transaction on a property — sales, loans, mortgages — over a chosen period, usually 13 or 30 years. A clean EC ("nil encumbrance") is one of the strongest signs a property isn't already mortgaged or entangled in an undisclosed loan.
How to check: Get the EC from the Sub-Registrar's office or the state registration portal for the maximum period available, not just a recent few years — old undisclosed loans can still surface later.
🏢
Occupancy Certificate (OC) / Completion Certificate (CC)
▾
An OC/CC from the local municipal authority certifies a building was constructed as per approved plans and is legally fit to occupy. Without it, a building is technically unauthorised, water/power connections can be denied, and banks may refuse a loan. It also affects GST — sale after a valid OC is GST-exempt; sale before OC is treated as a taxable "under-construction" service.
How to check: For a "ready to move" apartment, always ask to physically see the OC, not just take the builder's word. If it's genuinely under construction, ask for the RERA-registered expected completion date and the corresponding GST rate.
📐
Carpet Area vs Super Built-up Area
▾
Carpet Area is the actual usable floor space inside your walls — the number that legally matters. Super Built-up Area adds wall thickness, corridors, lobbies, and shared spaces, inflating the number a builder can advertise. The gap between the two is called the "loading factor," and it directly affects your real cost per square foot.
How to check: Under RERA Section 14(2), if the delivered carpet area is smaller than what was agreed, the builder must refund the difference with interest, typically within 45 days. Ask for the carpet area specifically, in writing, not just the super built-up figure used in marketing.
🛣️
Layout Approval (DTCP / HMDA / Municipal)
▾
For plots specifically, the entire layout — not just your individual plot — needs approval from the relevant planning authority (DTCP, HMDA, or local municipal body depending on location). An unapproved layout means no legal roads, drainage, or utility connections guaranteed, and banks generally won't finance a plot in one.
How to check: Ask for the Layout Approval Number and verify it with the issuing authority directly — not just trust a signboard or brochure claiming "approved layout."
🌾
Agricultural Land Conversion
▾
Land classified as agricultural cannot legally be used for residential or commercial construction until it's formally converted to non-agricultural (NA) status. Plots sold from unconverted agricultural land are a common source of long-term legal trouble, even if the sale itself completes without issue upfront.
How to check: Confirm the land classification directly on Bhu Bharati (or your state's land records portal) and ask specifically whether NA conversion has been completed and documented, not just promised.
⚖️
Pending Litigation Check
▾
Family disputes, inheritance claims, or bank recovery cases against a property don't always show up in a routine document check — but they can freeze or reverse your purchase years later.
How to check: A property lawyer can run a title search covering court records, not just registration records. For any significant purchase, this legal due diligence step is worth the cost relative to what's at stake.

TDS isn't an extra cost — you withhold it from the seller's payment and deposit it to the government via Form 26QB within 30 days, then give the seller Form 16B as proof.
💡 Tip: Under-declaring the sale value to save stamp duty is illegal and risky — it caps your resale documentation, invites tax scrutiny, and can be used against you in a dispute. Charges are on the higher of your value or guidance value regardless, so there's no real saving in trying.

Wrong GST ChargedBOTH
GST gets charged on a ready-to-move property that already has an Occupancy Certificate (should be GST-exempt), or the wrong rate is applied on an under-construction unit that actually qualifies for the lower affordable-housing rate.
Beat it: Ready-to-move with valid OC: 0% GST. Under-construction, affordable (≤₹45 lakh, carpet ≤60 sqm metro/90 sqm non-metro): 1%. Under-construction, other: 5%. Plain land sale: no GST at all. Check which bucket you're actually in before accepting the builder's number.

Your Legal Backing
RERA Act, 2016
Mandatory registration for projects over 500 sq.m or 8+ units (Section 3). 70% of buyer funds in escrow (Section 4). Advance capped at 10% before a sale agreement (Section 13). Carpet area shortfall refunded with interest (Section 14). Delay entitles refund or compensation (Section 18).
Telangana Bhu Bharati Act, 2025
Replaced the Dharani portal on 14 April 2025. Every land parcel has a 14-digit Bhudhaar ID. Section 22A lists land prohibited from registration — always check before paying.
GST on Real Estate
Ready-to-move with valid OC: exempt. Under-construction affordable housing (≤₹45L, carpet ≤60/90 sqm): 1%. Under-construction, other residential: 5%. Plain land sale: no GST.
